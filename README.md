Established biology
- Appearance cycle: Once every 300 years.
- Population: Several individuals.
- Distribution: Scattered across different regions.
- Species: One unique slime breed.
- Reproduction: Can produce hybrids with otherwise incompatible monster species.
- Individuality: Each member has its own personality, memories and behavioural traits.
Established abilities
1. Adaptive morphology
- Can change physical size.
- Can alter its body shape.
- Can imitate the general appearance of other monsters.
- Can blend into different monster populations.
- Can return to its original slime form.
2. Reproductive adaptation
Their unusual biology allows them to reproduce with otherwise incompatible monster species.

Their offspring may inherit:
- Physical characteristics from either parent.
- Unique slime-like biological properties.
- Unusual combinations of inherited traits.
We can establish the exact inheritance rules later.
3. Concealment
Their shape-changing ability also helps them avoid unwanted attention, particularly from humans.

Some may spend years disguised as ordinary monsters without being recognised.
4. Individual differences
Not every member uses these abilities in the same way. Some may prefer remaining in slime form, while others frequently change their appearance.

1. The Queen Slime
Central authority
The Queen Slime coordinates the activities of her slime population.

Her responsibilities include:
- Population distribution: Deciding which regions different slimes inhabit.
- Development: Coordinating how young slimes discover and develop their abilities.
- Knowledge: Managing the exchange of information between slime groups.
- Reproduction: Coordinating breeding and population continuity.
- Protection: Responding to threats against slime populations.
- Communication: Maintaining connections between geographically separated groups.
The Queen isn't simply a larger slime. She occupies a specialised position within their social and biological system.

3. The Queen's knowledge system
I particularly like your idea that she coordinates how slimes learn their abilities.

We could establish a system where:
- Slimes discover abilities through experimentation.
- Experienced slimes teach younger ones.
- Rare abilities are documented and shared.
- The Queen coordinates the distribution of specialised knowledge.
- Individual slimes can still develop abilities nobody has encountered before.
This also connects nicely with Ziggy's unusual adaptability.
Established rules
1. There can only be one reigning Queen Slime at a time.
2. The Queen possesses a special inherited biological trait.
3. The reigning Queen produces her successor.
4. The successor inherits the royal trait.
5. The successor eventually assumes leadership when the previous Queen's reign ends.
Established biological rules
1. The Queen can live indefinitely as long as her colony survives.
2. Her health and longevity are connected to the colony's overall condition.
3. She produces a successor carrying the inherited royal trait.
4. The colony maintains its social organisation through her leadership.
5. The Queen's death and succession are closely connected to the colony's survival.
Something interesting we could explore
The Queen might be capable of sensing the condition of individual slime populations across enormous distances.
Queen Slime: Emergency Succession
When the Queen dies unexpectedly
1. Temporary governance: The Elders take responsibility for coordinating the colony.
2. Successor protection: The newly produced Queen is protected by the colony.
3. Maturation: The successor develops her abilities and inherited royal traits.
4. Transfer of authority: Once mature, she assumes control of the entire slime population.
One important distinction
The colony doesn't necessarily become helpless without its Queen. Its Elders and Coordinators can maintain everyday operations.
